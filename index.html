Why weej exists

The work before the deliverable is still your work.

Years of remote consulting on government and corporate contracts kept surfacing the same problem: when a project ended, so did access to everything built around it — not the client's data, but the process behind it. The draft iterations. The templates. The prompt frameworks refined over dozens of engagements.

Create requirements or user stories directly inside a client's system, and you lose more than access when the contract closes. You lose the record of how your thinking got there — the approaches developed through trial and error that would've made the next engagement faster.

weej keeps the curation process local and private. Draft your prompts, extract your requirements, refine your stories on your own machine — then upload the finished work wherever it needs to go. The thinking stays yours.
